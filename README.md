# Vibe Coding - 1st prompt - High Level requirement
I am a heavy google users, I use Gmail for my main communication channel, Calender for my daily activities and payment reminder, Task for short term goals, Photos for capturing whatever I saw interesting during the day.
Now, I want to develop an agent system that can do:
1. Be my personal assistent and send me morning feed to remind me about my day plan.
2. Be my jounalist and report any highlight that comes from my email, calender and photos.
3. Be my writer and write a blog on what have I done and where I went for the day, and a weekly activity report.
And i want everything still within google's privacy boundray, and no PII data exchange between google and other AI platform.
